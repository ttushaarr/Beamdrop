BeamDrop is a free and open-source local Wi-Fi bridge between macOS and Android devices. It requires zero cloud relays, zero accounts, and works with 100% peer-to-peer privacy.

Features:
• Zero-Click File Transfers: Photos, videos, and documents sent from Mac auto-route into your Android Pictures, Movies, and Download folders with instant gallery indexing.
• 1-Click Wireless ADB: Pair and debug your device wirelessly over Wi-Fi without cables or typing IP/ports.
• Custom Mac Script Launchers: Turn your tablet or phone into a customizable Stream Deck to trigger Mac developer shell scripts (e.g. npm run dev, docker compose, git pull) and inspect live terminal output.
• Wireless HD Webcam: Stream your phone or tablet camera directly into OBS Virtual Cam, Zoom, and Google Meet at low latency.
• Incoming Call & SMS Alerts: View incoming calls on your Mac screen with 1-click decline and quick reply messages.
• Gemini AI Question Solver: Capture any question on Mac with ⌘⇧X and solve with Gemini AI in clear English on your tablet.
• Global Screenshot Beam: Press ⌘⇧D on Mac to beam Retina screenshots straight to your Android Pictures folder.
• Bidirectional Clipboard Sync: Instant copy-paste between devices over local Server-Sent Events (SSE).
• Remote Input & Trackpad: Wireless mouse cursor control with click, double-click, right-click, and keyboard typing.
• Media Remote & Presentation Clicker: Control Mac audio/video playback and slide decks remotely.
• Find My Device: Ring your lost tablet from Mac with maximum siren volume even when set to silent.
